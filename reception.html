Inspiring Greatness

The University of KwaZulu-Natal (UKZN) is committed to excellence in teaching, research, and community engagement.
With a rich history of fostering innovation and leadership, UKZN empowers students to achieve their full potential and make a meaningful impact in
South Africa and beyond. Our diverse and dynamic campus community embodies the spirit of curiosity, collaboration, and social responsibility, preparing
graduates to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

Courses We Offer

At the University of KwaZulu-Natal, we offer a diverse range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs designed to equip students with the knowledge, skills, and critical thinking needed to excel in their chosen fields. From science and engineering to humanities, business, and health sciences, our courses combine academic rigor with practical experience, preparing graduates to make a meaningful impact in society and the global workforce.

Honours Degrees

UKZN Honours programs provide students with an opportunity to deepen their knowledge and specialize in a particular field of study. These programs build on undergraduate learning, combining advanced coursework with research components that develop critical thinking, analytical skills, and professional competence. Completing an Honours degree prepares students for either professional practice or further postgraduate study

Masters Degrees

Master's programs at UKZN are designed for advanced study and research in a chosen discipline. They often combine coursework and independent research, allowing students to gain expertise, contribute original insights to their field, and develop the skills necessary for leadership and innovation. A Master's degree equips graduates for high-level professional roles or progression to doctoral studies.

Doctoral Degrees

Doctoral programs at UKZN represent the highest level of academic achievement, focused primarily on independent, original research. PhD candidates contribute new knowledge to their field, demonstrating advanced critical thinking, problem-solving, and scholarly rigor. This qualification prepares graduates for careers in academia, research, and leadership positions across diverse sectors globally.

About Us

The University of KwaZulu-Natal (UKZN) stands as a beacon of academic excellence and transformation in South Africa. Established on 1 January 2004, UKZN emerged from the merger of two prominent institutions: the University of Natal and the University of Durban-Westville. This strategic consolidation aimed to foster a more inclusive and dynamic higher education landscape in the province of KwaZulu-Natal. The University of Natal, founded in 1910 as the Natal University College in Pietermaritzburg, expanded to Durban in 1931.
Today, UKZN operates across five campuses:

  • Howard College (Durban)
  • Westville
  • Pietermaritzburg
  • Nelson R. Mandela School of Medicine
  • Edgewood
